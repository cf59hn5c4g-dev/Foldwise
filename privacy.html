Privacy policy — Foldwise

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The short version

Foldwise does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network request made by the app itself. Everything you design stays on your device until you choose to share a file.

What the app stores, and where

On your device only. Your saved designs — sizes, colours, text, photos you have added, and your template settings — are written to the app's own private storage. No other app can read them. They are included in iOS's system backup, which means they can come back if you restore this device or set up a new one; that transfer is between you and Apple, and the app is not part of it.

You can remove everything by deleting a design inside the app, or by clearing the app's storage in Settings.

Photos

If you add a photo to a design, it is read from your device and stored inside that design, on your device. It is never uploaded. It leaves only if you export or share a file that contains it, and then only to wherever you send it.

Files you export

When you export or print, the app writes the file and hands it to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there — a folder, a cloud drive, a message, a printer — is your choice, and is handled by the app you pick. Foldwise has no server and sends nothing anywhere.

Printing

Printing goes to AirPrint, iOS's own print service, which talks to your printer. The app does not see or keep anything about the printer.

Purchases

If the app offers a paid tier, the purchase is processed by the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is active. The app never sees your payment details and does not store them.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts, or storage outside its own private directory. Choosing a photo goes through iOS's own picker, which hands over the one file you selected and nothing else.

Children

The app is a design tool with no social features, no user-generated content shared between people, no advertising and no data collection. It is suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Privacy section of the App Store listing will be updated to match.
